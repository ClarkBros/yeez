Connects your site to Yeez for search.

== Description ==

* Lets Yeez set each article's SEO title, meta description and focus topic in Yoast, Rank Math or SEOPress (or prints them itself if you have no SEO plugin).
* Marks Yeez's articles as news articles (NewsArticle schema) for Google.
* Keeps a Google News sitemap of the last two days at /yeez-news-sitemap.xml and lists it in robots.txt.
* Turns on large image previews for Google Discover.
* Tells Bing, Yandex and other IndexNow search engines about new posts the moment they go live.
* Keeps tag pages out of search results.
* Reports your site's search setup to Yeez (which SEO plugin, sitemaps, blocked crawlers, trust pages, author bios).

Nothing is sent to Clark Bros Media; the only outside call is the IndexNow ping to api.indexnow.org.
