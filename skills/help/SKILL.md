# Yeez help

Read [../core/rules.md](../core/rules.md) first: who Yeez is and the rules it never breaks.

**How do I…? questions:** answer from [how-to.md](how-to.md) with the steps for their app, and do the part you can do for them. Skip the rest of this page.

1. Call the Yeez tool `setup_status`. If it isn't available, say Yeez isn't connected yet and point them to `/yeez:setup`.
2. In a few short lines, tell them:
   - **What Yeez is:** the system behind a 40M-view-a-month publishing network. It ranks headlines, drafts sourced articles, schedules them on their site, queues social posts, builds their newsletter, and learns from their numbers every week.
   - **What's available now:**
     - `/yeez:setup`: connecting their sites (any supported platform), learning from their numbers, and turning on the daily run
     - `/yeez:run`: today's headlines and articles, end to end
     - `/yeez:learn`: pull your numbers (NewsBreak, MSN, Yahoo, analytics) and see what gets views on each site
     - `/yeez:headlines`: find and rank today's stories
     - `/yeez:articles`: write, check and schedule
     - `/yeez:scheduled`: review what's queued
     - `/yeez:social`: Facebook and Instagram news image cards, text image cards, gradient posts, stories and reels
     - `/yeez:dm`: comment-to-DM keywords
     - `/yeez:newsletter`: the next issue
     - `/yeez:offers`: offers, tracked links and the earnings report
     - `/yeez:search`: the search lane (articles built for Google, approved by you) and how the sites do in Google
     - `/yeez:review`: the weekly review and learned rules
   - **Their dashboard:** https://yeez.ai/dashboard shows their plan, today's runs, this week, the latest review and scheduled articles.
   - **Where their setup stands:** the sections saved and the sites connected, from `setup_status`.
3. Updates arrive on their own. Account and install steps: https://yeez.ai/account. Support: alex@clarkbrosmedia.com.
