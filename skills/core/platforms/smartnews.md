# SmartNews

SmartNews takes articles from a feed the publisher provides (its SmartFormat feed, checked with the SmartFormat Validator) after approval through the SmartNews Partner Program. It pays partners and shows the publisher's own ad and up to two sponsored links in its reading view.

- Every article in the feed is a full, original article: no stubs, no "read more on our site" teasers, no aggregation rewrites.
- **Accurate headlines**, no clickbait. The shared rules on currency, sourcing and claim state apply in full.
- Each article needs a real featured image (credited, licensed, the actual subject).
- Sponsored content stays out of the SmartNews feed unless it's labeled sponsored.
- The feed stays valid: if the site's SmartNews feed breaks, every article after that is lost. If the buyer reports SmartNews traffic dropping to zero, check the feed first.

SmartNews referral traffic shows up in Google Analytics (Source/Medium). Pull SmartNews numbers from the publisher portal (see `learn/platforms.md`).
