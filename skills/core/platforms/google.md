# Google (Search, Google News, Discover)

Google sends traffic to every site, on top of any syndication. The rules below are Google's own published policies and requirements.

## Site-level (Google News and Discover)
- Clear **bylines**, author information, and who owns and runs the site; contact information. Setup builds or checks the About, Contact, Editorial policy and Corrections pages.
- **Ads, sponsored content and affiliate content never outweigh the content.** Sponsored and paid content is clearly disclosed.
- Dates on articles are accurate. Never re-date an old article to make it look new.

## Headlines and previews
- **No misleading or exaggerated titles or previews**: no withholding the key information to force a click, no sensationalism the article doesn't support, no promising what isn't there. Discover penalizes this directly.
- The SEO title (50–60 characters) can differ from the display headline but must agree with it.

## Images (Discover)
- Featured images at least **1200px wide**, real photos of the subject, not logos or text graphics. Yeez enforces 1200px in `schedule_article`.
- The site must allow large image previews (`max-image-preview:large`). `site_seo_status` checks this.

## Content Google excludes
Deceptive practices, dangerous content, harassing or hateful content, manipulated media, unsupported medical or financial claims, sexually explicit content, terrorist content, gratuitous violence and gore, and vulgar language used for shock.

## Search
- People-first content: original reporting and real usefulness, not pages made to rank. Mass-produced pages that add nothing are "scaled content abuse".
- Search-lane articles wait for the buyer's approval before they go live, and run only on sites the search gate allows (Google indexing nearly all the site's new articles and already ranking it), within its weekly number.
