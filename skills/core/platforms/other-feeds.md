# Flipboard, Apple News and other feeds

The shared rules cover these in full. What each adds:

## Apple News
- Accurate, non-clickbait headlines; opinion and commentary labeled.
- Every article needs a thumbnail image and an excerpt.
- Sponsored or native-advertising content is never sent as editorial.
- If the publisher sends notifications: 10–95 characters, no clickbait, nothing on thin or unconfirmed sourcing, no marketing, at most two non-breaking alerts a day. Repeated violations lose notification access.
- Corrections are clearly labeled.

## Flipboard
- Flipboard reads the site's RSS feed. Full, original articles with a real featured image and an accurate headline get picked up; teasers and clickbait don't.
- Keep the site's feed healthy (full text or a substantial excerpt, the featured image included).

## Any other platform
If the buyer is on a platform without its own file here, apply the shared rules and the stricter of the NewsBreak and MSN rules. In setup, read that platform's published content rules once and save anything specific in the site profile (`save_site_profile`, in `voice_notes` or `avoid`).
