# MSN (Microsoft Start partner)

MSN pays on page views, and **galleries pay there** (a gallery page view earns at a different rate from an article page view, and more items earn more). Sites on MSN can run galleries alongside articles when the site's `galleries_per_day` is above 0.

## Headlines
- Accurate and specific to what the article delivers. No clickbait, no sensationalism, no exaggeration.
- **No exclamation points.** No all-caps words for emphasis.
- No questions the article can't answer; no "you won't believe".
- Gallery titles start with the item count, and the gallery has exactly that many items.

## Content
- Original work by the partner; no aggregation rewrites, no duplicated articles under other brands. One brand per partner account; no duplicate brands.
- **Full-name bylines** from real authors.
- **AI-assisted content must be reviewed by a person and carry the AI-assisted disclosure.** Yeez adds the disclosure line; the buyer stays responsible for review (the site's `publish_mode` can be set to drafts if they want to read everything first).
- No ads, advertorial or sponsored content inside the article body sent to MSN. Affiliate or offer links: only where the partner agreement allows, and labeled.
- No YouTube embeds.
- News sites are expected to publish steadily (around 10 or more articles a day for news partners).

## Images
- The partner must hold the rights to every image, and every image is credited. **In galleries, every item's photo has its own credit.**
- No graphic images (human remains, gore, violence or death). Show the non-graphic, on-topic stand-in.
- Real photos of the actual subject. No artist renders, size-comparison charts or drawings presented as the thing.

## Galleries
- 8–14 items (target 12), each a real, named, photographable thing with its own specific source page.
- Never reuse a neighbouring item's source link. A page about one specific car, product or place must be that exact thing.
- No pre-photography events (nothing before about 1900 in that place), no events with no physical subject (blackouts, price moves), no subjects that only exist as drawings or renders.
- `schedule_gallery` checks the count, the word bands, one source link per item and source variety; follow what it refuses.

## Why items get rejected
Misleading or clickbait headlines, image rights problems or missing credits, missing bylines, graphic imagery, ads in the body, duplicated content, and AI content without the disclosure.
