# Facebook and Instagram

Meta removed the founding network's posts under Fraud, Scams and Deceptive Practices, suspended a Page, restricted the managing profile, and suspended recommendations on new Pages. These rules are why. The house style is in `social/format-rules.md`; these are the survival rules on top.

## 1. Money-safety (the rule that fired)
Money, tax, benefit and policy stories stay, with the number, the actor and the fight intact. Report them in the **third person, as news**: "the law creates a $6,000 deduction for people 65 and older, 2025–2028". Never frame it as the viewer personally collecting government money.

**Banned wording (rewrite on sight):** "money in your account/pocket", "every dollar back in your pocket", "money you're owed", "claim yours / what's yours", "free money", "get/keep $X", "you qualify / you're eligible for $X", "make it permanent?" as a money call to action, "now law / signed into law" used as a personal windfall, any urgency + money + "you" combination. No loan, insurance, debt-relief or "the government will pay/forgive you" promises. No giveaway, cash-flip or "register to claim" mechanics.

A call to action offers a tool or a product by name, never a windfall, a price promise, eligibility or an outcome.

## 2. Accuracy at creation
Every number sourced, every quote verbatim, every event real and current, every claim state checked, before the post is written (the shared rules). A misattributed or stale-as-current claim is dropped. Community Notes can attach to a technically-true-but-misleading post and kill its reach, so avoid misleading framing, not just false facts.

## 3. Never look like a network of copies
Meta targets "multiple accounts used in concert" and repetitive posting. One flagged pattern can cascade to every Page.
- **Repost cap:** a concept runs at most **3 times per Page, ever**, and never verbatim. Every copy is genuinely reworded (wording, number, angle, visual) across Pages and across reposts. Only genuinely new news resets the count. Plain black-background text posts are the one format that may repeat word for word, still within the cap.
- Spreading a winning concept across many Pages is good, as long as each Page gets its own version.
- Vary the call-to-action sentence; the same line on every post is a spam signal.
- No identical card fanned across all Pages on the same night.

## 4. New Pages: warm them up
Of 14 Pages created in two days, the first nine lost recommendations (Meta stops suggesting them to non-followers, the growth engine of a new Page). Recommendation rules are stricter than Community Standards, so a Page can lose them with "no violations".
- No more than 2 new Pages a week, spread across days.
- A new Page gets **7 days, 10–15 original posts, zero links, zero calls to action** before Yeez's full schedule starts. When a Page is connected with under 100 followers and fewer than 15 posts (`get_social_brief`: `followers`, `recent_posts`), the social skill sets `warmup_until` a week out with `save_social_settings`; while the brief shows `warmup`, the Page gets warm-up posts only.
- Keep the link share low on a young Page; no comment-bait ("comment YES") while young.
- Never delete and recreate a suspended Page; keep posting good original content. Suspended recommendations are re-evaluated on recent content and usually return.

## 5. Everything else Meta enforces
- **Hateful conduct:** criticize policies, records and actions, never a protected group as a group. No story blames a nationality or group.
- **Violence and incitement:** intense is fine; threats or wishes of harm are not.
- **Bullying and privacy:** never target a private person; never show anyone's address, phone, account or private documents.
- **Elections:** dates, results and voting procedures always correct.
- **Intellectual property:** rebuild a viral idea on your own asset with your own words; never re-upload another Page's image or a watermarked photo. Credit every photo.
- No emoji in Yeez posts. Short-hook reels never say "read the caption" or "breakdown below".

## Final check before every post
Accurate and current · no banned money wording · not a dropped concept · no attack on a protected group · no threats, no private person, no personal info · not misleading · this Page's own version, within the cap · our own image, credited. Any miss: rewrite or drop the slot. Never fill a slot with filler.
