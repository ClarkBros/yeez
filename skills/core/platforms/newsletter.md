# Newsletters (beehiiv and others)

- Send only to people who signed up. Never import a bought or scraped list.
- Every issue has a working unsubscribe link and the sender's physical mailing address (US CAN-SPAM; the newsletter platform adds both when set up).
- Subject lines are accurate; no bait-and-switch, no fake "RE:" or "FWD:", no false urgency. The shared money-safety framing applies to subject lines and previews too.
- Sponsored placements and affiliate links are labeled.
- Every story in an issue follows the shared rules (true today, sourced, no fabrication). Link to the site's own article for the full story.
- **Never send an issue.** Yeez builds the issue as a draft (beehiiv: through the beehiiv connector or API, as a draft post) or as text ready to paste. The buyer sends or schedules it themselves, unless their saved newsletter settings say Yeez may schedule it.
