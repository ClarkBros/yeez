# Yahoo (content partners and Yahoo for Creators)

Yahoo distributes partner and creator articles across the Yahoo News network and shares ad revenue on them. Partners are vetted, and everything is held to Yahoo's content standards: credible, trusted, quality work.

- **Accurate headlines** that the article fully delivers. No clickbait or bait-and-switch.
- **Original** articles; no aggregation rewrites or copied text. Rewrites of wire copy don't count as original.
- **Sourced and attributed**, primary sources first (the shared rules).
- **Current.** Yahoo News surfaces news; stale-as-current stories are the fastest way to lose distribution.
- Opinion is labeled as opinion; sponsored or affiliate content is labeled as such.
- Images are licensed for this use and credited.
- Real, full-name bylines.
- No hate, harassment, graphic violence, adult content, dangerous misinformation, or health and financial claims the sources don't support.

Yahoo's numbers come from the partner or creator dashboard the buyer was given when approved (see `learn/platforms.md`). Judge by views, never by click-through rate.

If the buyer's partner agreement has specific rules (headline length, categories, image sizes, how many articles a day), they override this file. Yeez learns them in setup, and they're saved in the site profile.
